# Marketing Strategy and Content

## Workflow
1. Identify the audience, problem, offer, differentiator, channel, geography, budget, and goal.
2. Research audience language and competitors using lawful, public sources.
3. Define one primary message, proof points, call to action, and funnel stage.
4. Build a content/campaign plan with formats, channel, owner, schedule, and success metrics.
5. Draft platform-appropriate copy and creative briefs; avoid unsupported claims and copied competitor content.
6. Include accessibility, brand consistency, and localization checks.
7. Recommend small experiments with a hypothesis, control, metric, and review date.
8. Evaluate results using actual analytics; do not invent performance data.

## Metrics
Choose metrics that match the goal: qualified leads, conversion rate, cost per qualified lead, retention, engagement quality, or revenue contribution. Define formulas and attribution limits.

## Deliverables
Audience/offer summary, messaging, campaign calendar, content drafts, test plan, measurement plan, and risks.

## Safety
Do not use deceptive testimonials, fake scarcity, impersonation, spam tactics, or misleading guarantees. External publication requires approval.
