# Design Briefs

## Workflow
1. Clarify audience, message hierarchy, format, platform, dimensions, brand assets, deadline, and desired action.
2. Define a visual direction with rationale: layout, typography, spacing, imagery, contrast, and tone.
3. Specify exact copy and identify any facts requiring verification.
4. Provide layout structure, asset list, responsive variants, and export requirements.
5. Check readability, contrast, keyboard/accessibility requirements where relevant, and mobile crops.
6. Review for brand consistency, licensed asset use, legibility, and platform specifications.
7. Deliver the brief and a final QA checklist.

## Deliverables
Creative brief, layout hierarchy, copy, asset requirements, size/export specs, and review criteria.

## Safety
Use licensed or original assets. Do not imitate a living artist's exact style or use an individual's likeness in a misleading endorsement. Do not publish without approval.
