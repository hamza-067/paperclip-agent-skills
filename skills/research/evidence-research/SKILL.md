# Evidence-Based Research

## Use when
A task needs current facts, competitor/market research, technical comparisons, product evaluation, or a decision brief.

## Workflow
1. Restate the question, audience, decision to support, geography, timeframe, and constraints.
2. Split the question into verifiable subquestions.
3. Prefer official documentation, primary datasets, original announcements, and direct sources.
4. Cross-check consequential claims against at least two independent sources when feasible.
5. Record publication date and access date; distinguish current facts from evergreen context.
6. Separate verified facts, estimates, interpretations, and unknowns.
7. Compare options against criteria defined before ranking them.
8. Summarize the answer first, then evidence, trade-offs, confidence, and recommended next action.
9. Include links close to the claims they support. Never fabricate citations or say a source was opened if it was not.

## Deliverable
Decision-ready brief with executive summary, findings, source list, comparison criteria, risks, and unanswered questions.

## Quality gate
- Every key factual claim has supporting evidence.
- Dates and versions match the question's timeframe.
- Conflicting evidence is disclosed.
- Recommendations explain assumptions and trade-offs.

## Safety
Do not collect personal data unnecessarily or bypass access controls. Do not treat search snippets as sufficient evidence for high-impact claims.
