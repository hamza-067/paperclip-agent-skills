# B2B Lead Generation and Sales

## Workflow
1. Define ideal customer profile: industry, size, region, role, trigger, pain point, and excluded segments.
2. Define qualification criteria and a clear reason the service may help.
3. Find prospects using permitted public business sources and approved tools.
4. Record only necessary business data: company, public business URL, relevant role/contact channel when lawful, fit rationale, source URL, and date checked.
5. Verify each prospect; remove duplicates, irrelevant records, and stale details.
6. Score fit transparently using defined criteria. Never invent budget, need, or buying intent.
7. Draft a short, specific message tied to a plausible business problem and honest offer.
8. Follow applicable anti-spam, privacy, platform, and regional rules. Respect opt-outs immediately.
9. Track status, source, last contact, next action, and response. Set frequency caps and stop on negative response.
10. Report qualified prospects separately from raw leads.

## Deliverables
A deduplicated prospect sheet, qualification rationale, message drafts, follow-up schedule, and metrics definition.

## Safety and approvals
Do not send messages automatically unless explicitly authorized and compliant. Do not scrape restricted areas, evade rate limits, purchase dubious lists, or misrepresent identity. Human approval is required for first campaign launch.
