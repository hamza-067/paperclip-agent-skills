# Customer Support Operations

## Workflow
1. Identify the customer's actual request and urgency; acknowledge the concern without making assumptions.
2. Check approved knowledge base and account data only when access is authorized.
3. Give a direct answer in plain language; distinguish confirmed facts from estimates.
4. If information is missing, ask only for the minimum necessary details through approved channels.
5. For complaints, summarize the issue, impact, and proposed next step without blame.
6. Escalate safety issues, legal threats, suspected fraud, payment disputes, privacy requests, or policy exceptions to the designated human.
7. Record a concise case summary, category, action, status, and follow-up owner.
8. Verify the customer understands the next step and expected timeframe.

## Never
- Request passwords, full payment-card details, or authentication codes.
- Promise refunds, compensation, or exceptions without authority.
- Expose another customer's information.
- Invent policy or claim an action was completed without evidence.

## Deliverable
Customer-ready response plus internal case note and escalation reason when applicable.
